# History essay draft

For the history essay draft, write an arguable thesis before drafting the body. Use primary sources as evidence, distinguish an author's claim from your own interpretation, and connect each paragraph back to the thesis.

Plan the history essay in three stages: outline the argument, draft paragraphs with evidence, then revise the introduction and conclusion. Keep a bibliography while writing and cite quotations and paraphrases. Check the assignment's required citation style before submitting.
